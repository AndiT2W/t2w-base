## Summary

- The product should also contain an invoicing tool.
- For individual Time2Win events, there should be a project management process plan.
- The system should provide templates for recurring task groups such as bib numbers, registration, and timing.
- A strong overview of the critical path is especially important.
- The system should show overdue tasks clearly and indicate where escalation is needed.
- Tasks can be assigned to Time2Win employees, Time2Win groups, or the organizer side.

## Key Facts

- Event delivery is a repeatable operational process.
- Task templates should support recurring operational domains.
- Critical-path visibility is a first-class operational requirement.
- Assignment targets are broader than just internal users.
- Organizer-side deliverables, such as bib design, must be assignable and trackable.

## Implications For Project

- The project-management module must support reusable templates, dependencies, and deadline monitoring.
- The assignment model must support internal users, internal groups, and external organizer contacts.
- The roadmap should include billing or invoicing as a scoped module.
- Delivery dashboards should highlight urgent blockers instead of only listing tasks.
